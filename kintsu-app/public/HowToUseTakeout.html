Connect Your Data Securely

Kintsu needs access to your receipts and order confirmations to process your claims. Follow this guide to provide a filtered export from Google Takeout instead of giving full email access.

⚡ Important Pre-Step: Create a Label

Before exporting, we need to group the relevant emails together in Gmail.

  1. Open Gmail.
  2. In the search bar, paste this exact search:
in:anywhere (receipt OR order OR "sales invoice") OR label:(^cob_sm_order OR ^cob_sm_cl_jc_order OR ^cob_sm_cl_llm_order)
  1. Click the checkmark box in the top left to Select All.
    (Note: Ensure you click "Select all conversations that match this search" if it appears).
  2. Click the Labels icon → Create new.
  3. Name it Kintsu_Export and click Create.
1
Go to Google Takeout

Open your browser and navigate to the official Google data export tool.

Open Google Takeout →

Make sure you are signed in to the correct Google account.

2
Deselect Everything

By default, Google selects all your data (Photos, Drive, Maps, etc.). We don't want that.

Look for the "Select data to include" section and click the button at the top of the list.

[Screenshot: "Deselect all" button highlighted]
3
Select Only "Mail"
  1. Scroll down the list until you find "Mail".
  2. Check the box next to "Mail".
[Screenshot: "Mail" checkbox selected]
4
Filter Your Data (Crucial Step!)

We only want specific emails (receipts & orders). Filtering reduces the file size and protects your privacy.

  1. Click the button that says "All Mail data included".
  2. Uncheck "Include all messages in Mail".
  3. Check ONLY the Kintsu_Export label you created in the Pre-Step.
  4. Click OK.
[Screenshot: Label selection menu showing only "Kintsu_Export" checked]
5
Create Export
  1. Scroll to the very bottom and click "Next step".
  2. Destination: Select "Send download link via email".
  3. Frequency: Select "Export once".
  4. File type & size: Leave as .zip and 2 GB.
  5. Click "Create export".
6
Upload to Kintsu

Wait for the email from Google (this usually takes a few minutes for filtered exports).

  1. Download the .zip file from the email.
  2. Drag and drop that file into the Kintsu Drop Zone below.
